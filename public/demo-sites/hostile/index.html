Our story

Ember & Oak started in 2019 with a ten-kilo roaster in a railway arch. We still roast by hand, one batch at a time, and taste every batch before it leaves.

Where our beans come from

Every bag is single-origin and traceable to the farm. We buy directly from three partners and pay above Fairtrade prices.

Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia

Jasmine, bergamot and stone fruit.

Huila, Colombia

Red apple, panela and cocoa.

Minas Gerais, Brazil

Hazelnut, milk chocolate and cherry.
